What we heard

The brief

A macOS menu-bar companion for Dota 2: locks the cursor, blocks Mac shortcuts and remaps modifier keys. For Mac players who remember DotA 1. It should feel nostalgic and native to the Mac.

nostalgicnative Mac

Success: keeps the Necronomicon and ⌘, reads at 16 px in the menu bar, works in one colour, and stays clear of Valve's shield.
